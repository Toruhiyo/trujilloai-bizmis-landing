# ad-1

Played at `/?marketing=ad-1`. The default call to action is `cta=demo`. `cta=ea`, `cta=install`, and `cta=none` change only the end card. `part=pain` stops after the dull sea. `part=pitch` starts at the switch.

Lines in quotes are either on screen or spoken by the clerk. Spoken lines are not written on the store.

## 1. A store with nobody selling

A desktop window titled "Your store". A product grid, no clerk. The shopper opens a product, goes back, scrolls, and opens another.

## 2. Dull Chatbot

A chat panel opens, titled "Dull Chatbot". Chips: Track order, Returns, Contact us.

The shopper types: "Looking for something light I can take everywhere."

The bot answers: "You can browse our full collection. Use the filters to narrow by size and weight." Links: View collection, Size guide.

The shopper types: "Which one would you pick for me?"

The bot answers: "Recommendations vary by preference. Check each product page for details, or I can open a support ticket." Buttons: Open a ticket, No, thanks.

## 3. Close the chat, then LOST

The window stays flat and full frame. A cursor moves to the chat's X and clicks. The chat closes. A grey veil covers the window and "LOST" stamps in grey. The veil stays inside the rounded window.

## 4. The dull sea

That same window is the first card. The camera pulls back over a sea of store cards. Most of them stamp "LOST". While the sea is still moving and blurred, this line comes up and stays:

"Your store, on a typical day."

The sea washes to solid light grey. The line stays on top.

## 5. The switch

The sea fades out. A switch reads "Typical chatbot" and "Agentic sales". The knob flips to Agentic sales. That label moves to center, grows, and bursts to white.

## 6. Your store sales agent

The Bizmis mark. Then the line, word by word: "Your store salesperson."

"person" is struck out and replaced, so the line reads "Your store sales agent."

Then, one word at a time: "Built." "to." "sell."

## 7. The clerk sells

The clerk sits in the corner of the store. Four beats, spoken:

1. "I narrow it down to the best few." The catalog fades in.
2. "I recommend the one that fits them best." Two products are compared. The right one gets the tick.
3. "I clear them like an expert. And close the deal." A product page, question marks around the photo, then the close.
4. "I suggest what goes best with it." A matching extra, then the bundle, with a check that draws itself.

## 8. The selling sea

The product window is marked sold: an orange veil and a white check. The camera pulls back over a sea of stores. While it is still moving and blurred, this line comes up and stays:

"Built to sell."

The sea washes to solid Bizmis orange. The white Bizmis mark and the line stay on top.

## 9. Other stores

A wave of real stores passes. The clerk goes with them.

## 10. End card

White, centered. The Bizmis wordmark first. Then, by `cta`:

- `demo` (the default): "See it in action" and `bizmis.ai/demo`
- `ea`: "First 50 stores. Free to run live." then "Join Early Access" and `bizmis.ai/early-access`
- `install`: "Install on Shopify". No URL.
- `none`: the wordmark only.

The claim is solid ink with a moving shine. It holds, then the film leaves.
