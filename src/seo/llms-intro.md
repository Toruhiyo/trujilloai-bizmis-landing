# Bizmis

> Bizmis is a voice-first AI sales agent for Shopify stores. It sits in the storefront as a small widget with a 3D avatar, talks with shoppers out loud, narrows the catalog to the products that fit, answers their doubts on the spot from the store's own details, and walks them to checkout. It's not a chatbot.

Bizmis is for Shopify merchants who lose sales in the moments an in-store clerk would catch: shoppers who can't find the right product, shoppers stuck comparing options, and buyers with a full cart who hesitate. Whatever the store sells, the same agent also answers support questions day and night and turns every conversation into a clear next step for the store. Shoppers can speak or type, and Bizmis answers out loud while the widget shows a transcript. It installs from the Shopify App Store in one click and keeps itself in sync with the store.
