## 1. Getting Started

### What is Bizmis?

Bizmis is a fully-integrated, plug-and-play digital sales assistant for Shopify stores. It welcomes shoppers, answers questions, and confidently guides them to purchase decisions—like a great in-store salesperson.

### Is Bizmis a chatbot?

No. Bizmis is not a chatbot. It's a professional, voice-powered sales and support presence embedded in your store.

### How fast can I get started?

Installation is one click from the Shopify App Store. Bizmis auto-syncs with your store and works immediately—no manual configuration and no training required.

### When will I start seeing value?

From first install to first sale—Bizmis goes live and starts helping customers immediately.

## 3. Billing

### How does the billing work?

Billing operates on monthly cycles. Your included minutes reset every month with no rollover. Voice usage is billed in minutes. The monthly commitment is charged upfront at the start of each month.

### What happens after I use my included minutes?

After your included minutes are used, additional minutes are billed at your plan's extra per-minute rate. You can set a spend hard limit. Usage is tracked in real time in your dashboard.

### How does yearly billing work?

Yearly billing gives you ~20% off (up to 33% with Early Bird). Included minutes still reset every month. The yearly commitment is charged upfront.

### What is the Early Bird offer?

The first 50 merchants receive special pricing: 50% off monthly plans for the first 3 months, or special yearly rates (up to 33% off). Extra minutes remain at standard rates.

### What's your refund policy?

Yearly plans include a 30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked (commitment fee only). Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime.

### Can I upgrade from monthly to yearly?

Yes! Upgrade to yearly within your first 90 days and we'll credit what you already paid toward your yearly plan. That credit applies to commit charges only; on-demand usage (extra minutes) is excluded.

### What happens when I upgrade my plan?

Upgrades take effect immediately. You're charged the prorated difference for the remaining time in your billing period, and your billing date stays the same. New features and higher limits are available right away.

### What happens when I downgrade my plan?

Downgrades take effect at your next renewal date — you keep your current plan and all its features until then. Your billing cycle and usage counters are not reset.
